      Full list ·

      "Saturday" is that week's own Saturday-morning-to-Sunday-morning storm reading. "% increase" is that Saturday's own kills as a percentage of the lifetime total they had going into that Saturday — how much that one storm grew their total by. "Other days" is the ordinary, non-storm days right before it (last Sunday morning to this Saturday morning). "Week" is the two added together — this Saturday's storm plus this week's ordinary days — so Saturday + Other days = Week on every row.

      Averages are worked out as of the week picked above, walking backward through that member's own Saturday-kills history and skipping any week they have no reading for (left mid-storm, joined recently, a missed pull) rather than counting a blank week as zero or breaking the average. "Rolling 4-week" uses their last 4 weeks with a reading, however far back that takes it; "All-time" uses every reading they have. Members with no usable readings yet as of that week are left out.

      Rules

      To be entered at all, your rolling 4-week average (Saturday kills) must be above 199,999.

      If you clear that bar, you score points for that week's own Saturday kills:

      • 1 point for kills above 200,000 that Saturday
      • +1 more (2 total) for kills above 500,000
      • +1 more (3 total) for kills above 800,000
      • +1 more if you're one of the top 10 eligible members by % increase that week (a fixed top 10, not 10% of the pool) — this stacks on top of 0 or 1 point from kills (so 200k+ plus a top-10 improver = 2), but does not stack once you're already at 500k+ or 800k+ — those stay at 2 or 3.

      Everyone who scored at least 1 point goes into a weighted random draw for 3 winners — a 3-point member is 3× as likely to be drawn as a 1-point member. Anyone who won in either of the last 2 weeks is left out of the draw (a 2-week cooldown after winning), and anyone on the exclusion list (below) is left out entirely, regardless of points.

      Changes

      Profession comes from the same automated pull as kills — a reading is taken every Saturday and Sunday run, no extra API calls needed. This tab only shows people currently in the alliance — anyone who's left since the last pull drops off here (they're untouched everywhere else in the app, including their kill history). "Changes" only counts an actual switch between two known professions — a member's first-ever reading is never counted as a change.

      Recent runs

      Every successful automated pull (Friday backup, Saturday start, Sunday end) logs itself here — there's no "failed" status to show, a run that fails just never appears. If a row you're expecting isn't here, that run didn't happen: for a missing Saturday start, promote that week's backup from the GitHub Actions tab (workflow_dispatch, mode=promote_backup) before Sunday's end run happens.

      Date/Time is when the automation finished saving that reading. Data age is different — it's how long ago LW Atlas itself confirmed the scan it was reading from had actually finished, i.e. how stale the underlying kill numbers are (hover it for the exact time). These are normally only seconds or minutes apart, since a fresh scan is always triggered and waited on before anything is read — if you ever see Data age showing hours or days on a recent run, that's worth flagging. It shows "—" for runs from before this was tracked (2026-08-31) or for backup/professions-only runs, which don't go through a triggered scan.

      Search
      Saturday-morning pulls, per person

      Each row is a raw reading pulled automatically on Saturday morning, before that week's storm — a running lifetime-kills total for that person at that point in time, not a weekly figure. A new row gets added for everyone each week as the automated pull keeps running, newest first.

      Weekly manual pull (CSV upload)

      Use this whenever the automated pull can't run. Upload a CSV with one member per line as Name,Kills — their lifetime kill total at that reading, not a weekly figure — once for the Saturday-morning "Start" reading and again for the Sunday-morning "End" reading. Everything downstream (Saturday kills, Other days, Week total, %) then gets computed automatically, exactly like the automated pull would. A header row is fine either way; kill numbers with or without thousand-separator commas both work.

      Add a historical week (one-off)

      For a one-off correction or an old week you only have a single "kills that Saturday" figure for — not the weekly CSV upload above. Paste one member per line as Name, Kills (or Name, Kills, PreviousTotal if you also know their total going into that Saturday, to get a % figure too). Lines starting with # are ignored.

      Create an event

      For ad-hoc alliance events (not the regular Saturday/Sunday storm) — a scan gets triggered automatically at the exact start time and again at the exact end time you set below, the same way the weekly pull works. Each scan takes a few minutes to complete once its moment arrives; results fill in below once both are done. Times are whatever your own browser considers local.

      One alliance per line, as AllianceID,Label (e.g. 12345,RVLS). Pre-filled with 1GN plus the 3 alliances events usually track alongside it — edit or delete lines for a one-off event. All alliances listed here are read from the same single scan — no extra cost per alliance.

      Event participation

      Based on every event that's had both its start and end readings captured so far. % participated is how often someone's kill count actually went up over an event's window; % over 10k is the same but only counting events where they gained more than 10,000 kills; avg kills/event averages their kill gain across every counted event, including the zeros. The small number under each name is how many events that's based on — treat a percentage based on only 1-2 events with some caution. Only counts events for 1GN's own roster (guest alliances tracked in an event aren't matched to a member here), and only visible (non-hidden) members.

      Connection

      Hidden members ·

      For people who've left the game, farm accounts only around for a short time, or anyone else you don't want cluttering the stats. Hiding someone removes them from the Dashboard, History, Professions, and Train tabs entirely — including Train eligibility and their past-winner history — and from the public dashboard. Their data stays exactly as it is in Supabase, untouched; unhide them any time and everything picks back up.
